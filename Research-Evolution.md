  I will present this investment analysis to the fund’s investment committee. The decision is whether the fund should

  ## Initial Thesis: Watch-Defer

  My initial recommendation is watch-defer.

  NVIDIA demonstrated exceptional revenue, profitability, and operating cash-flow performance in fiscal 2026. Its Data
  Center business is the principal operating-value driver, supported by demand for accelerated computing and artificial-
  intelligence infrastructure. NVIDIA also reported more cash and marketable securities than debt.

  However, strong company performance does not automatically make the stock attractive at every price. I do not yet have
  the historical market price, enterprise value, valuation multiples, or a completed discounted cash-flow analysis as of
  February 25, 2026. The company also faces customer concentration, large supply and cloud commitments, export
  restrictions, competition, and uncertainty about whether recent growth can be sustained.

  I would defer initiation until valuation analysis shows that the expected return provides an adequate margin of safety
  relative to these risks.

  ## Consequential Assumptions

  1. AI infrastructure demand remains durable.
     The thesis assumes that demand reflects a lasting increase in computing requirements rather than temporary over-
     ordering.

  2. Data Center growth remains economically attractive.
     Future revenue must generate sustainable margins and cash flow, not merely additional sales volume.

  3. NVIDIA maintains a competitive advantage.
     The company must continue differentiating its hardware, networking, software, and developer ecosystem despite
     competition and customer-developed chips.

  4. Supply commitments convert into profitable sales.
     NVIDIA’s large manufacturing and capacity commitments assume sufficient future demand. A demand slowdown could
     result in excess inventory, purchase-obligation charges, or weaker margins.

  5. Export restrictions remain manageable.
     The analysis assumes regulatory restrictions will not eliminate a materially larger portion of NVIDIA’s addressable
     market.

  6. Customer concentration does not materially weaken NVIDIA’s position.
     Major customers must continue purchasing NVIDIA products without gaining enough bargaining power to substantially
     reduce NVIDIA’s margins.

  7. The market price permits an adequate expected return.
     Even optimistic operating results may produce a poor investment if the stock price already reflects more aggressive
     expectations.

  ## Important Unknowns

  - NVIDIA’s closing share price and market capitalization on February 25, 2026
  - Enterprise value on the valuation date
  - The amount of dilution not fully captured by basic shares outstanding
  - Sustainable long-term Data Center growth
  - Sustainable gross and operating margins
  - Normalized free cash flow after capital expenditures
  - Appropriate weighted average cost of capital
  - Appropriate terminal-growth rate
  - Customer capital-spending plans and the possibility of overbuilding AI capacity
  - The competitive effect of custom chips and competing accelerators
  - The financial effect of current and possible future export restrictions
  - Whether the Groq transaction will generate returns above its cost
  - The probability that supply, capacity, and cloud commitments become underutilized
  - A reasonable valuation range under downside, base, and upside scenarios

  ## Research Plan

  1. Obtain NVIDIA’s adjusted closing share price on February 25, 2026 from a reliable historical market-data source.
  2. Reconcile basic shares outstanding, diluted weighted-average shares, equity awards, and other potential dilution
     before estimating equity value.

  3. Calculate market capitalization and enterprise value using consistent valuation-date inputs.
  4. Extract at least three years of revenue, operating income, operating cash flow, capital expenditures, and relevant
     segment results from NVIDIA’s SEC filings.

  5. Calculate historical growth, margins, free cash flow, and returns on invested capital while checking the treatment
     of stock-based compensation and acquisition costs.

  6. Build downside, base, and upside scenarios for Data Center revenue growth, operating margins, reinvestment, and
     free cash flow.

  7. Construct a discounted cash-flow valuation using explicit assumptions for growth, margins, reinvestment, discount
     rate, and terminal value.

  8. Compare NVIDIA with relevant semiconductor and computing-infrastructure companies using consistently defined
     enterprise-value and equity-value multiples.

  9. Stress-test the valuation for slower AI spending, customer losses, lower margins, export restrictions, excess
     capacity, increased competition, and additional dilution.

  10. Establish an initiation price that offers an appropriate margin of safety and identify evidence that would cause
     the thesis to be revised or rejected.

## Methods and results are version-specific

The saved Lab 06 independent FCFF-growth DCF produced about **$60.00**. Lab 10's FCFE forecast produced **$62.97**; Lab 11's Data Center-only operating sensitivity spanned **$54.03-$73.29**. These are preserved historical results, not alternate outputs of the final runner.

The revised runner derives FCFF from the statements, corrects the depreciation treatment and uses an explicit operating-cash reserve and enterprise-to-equity bridge. Its **$66.22** base and **$50.27-$87.17** combined-case range supersede those figures for the current project demonstration. Changing several model conventions means the difference is not attributable solely to a single driver.

## One material output corrected and independently checked

The earlier generated pro-forma applied gross margin and functional-expense ratios, then deducted depreciation again. The correction is to include depreciation in those operating expense measures and add it back only when calculating operating cash flow/FCFF, while retaining it in the PP&E roll-forward.

The independent source check uses the FY2026 consolidated income statement: **153,463 - 18,497 - 4,579 = 130,387 million**. Those expenses already reconcile gross profit to reported operating income. A second standalone deduction is unsupported by that definition. **Disposition: corrected.** The named source concepts, filing hash and calculation are saved in `outputs/project1-operating-income-check.json`. Accounting/FCFF tests then check the revised calculation, without treating a passing test as proof of the forecast assumptions.

## How challenges affected the decision

Lab 11's growth-range test matters because five annual shifts compound; its ranking is limited to the selected ranges. Lab 12's challenge that discount-rate uncertainty remained untested changes research priorities: the final runner now includes WACC/terminal-growth sensitivity. The reverse DCF shows the demanding growth path needed to reconcile the saved market reference with the model if other assumptions remain fixed.

The qualified peer exercise supplies conflicting evidence rather than a valuation shortcut. Different earnings bases and business mixes explain why its multiples cannot be transferred mechanically. An enterprise-value peer calculation is still missing and is treated as an evidence gap, not as a completed cross-check.

## Current conditions and remaining uncertainty

The five-year forecast is the analytical horizon. The action is to defer a new position and review the next earnings evidence. A refreshed downside case near or above the relevant price, with supported cash-flow assumptions, dilution and discount rate, could support reconsideration. Persistent weak demand, margin pressure or unproductive reinvestment could support do not initiate.

The current range is conditional historical model output. It does not resolve later-quarter information, interim valuation-date timing, normalized base-year cash flows, point-in-time diluted shares, other equity claims or terminal reinvestment. That uncertainty is a reason to defer, not to disguise the range as a precise current price target.

**What changed, and why?** The recommendation did not change. The evidence, model integration, diagnostics and conditions became more explicit. The adopted conclusion is stronger as a conditional research decision, while the incomplete valuation evidence prevents claiming a fully investment-ready intrinsic-value range.
