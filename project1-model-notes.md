# Revised model definitions and evidence

## Scope

Target: NVIDIA common equity. Intended user: investment committee considering
its first position. Working action: watch/defer. Financial forecast: five annual
periods labeled FY2027-FY2031. Currency: USD; statements and shares in millions.
Accounting base: FY2026, ended January 25, 2026. Market reference: September 10,
2026 close, $218.36, inherited from Lab 08. No new price observation is claimed.
An explicit investment holding horizon still needs to be chosen by the analyst.

## Changes from the lab engines

1. FCFF is generated by the operating forecast, not an independently grown
   starting cash-flow number. Formula: after-tax operating income + depreciation
   - capital spending - change in modeled operating working capital.
2. FCFF uses WACC. It excludes interest expense, debt repayments, buybacks and
   dividends. These financing items remain in the statements and cash checks.
3. Reported gross margin and expense ratios already include depreciation.
   Depreciation is no longer subtracted a second time from operating profit.
   It remains in the PP&E roll-forward and cash-flow addback.
4. Cash and marketable securities are separate balance-sheet lines. Marketable
   securities are held constant; cash ties to operating, investing and financing
   flows. The operating cash floor is $10,000 million, a new explicit assumption.
5. Enterprise value is bridged to equity with cash + marketable securities,
   minus an operating reserve of $10,605 million, minus $8,468 million of debt.
   Treating the securities as nonoperating and reserving all opening cash are
   judgments, not verified fair-value adjustments for every holding/claim.
6. Valuation uses 24,514 million annual diluted weighted-average shares as a
   fixed proxy. This is not a complete point-in-time diluted share schedule.

## What the automatic checks establish

The engine checks opening and forecast balance-sheet identities, cash-flow
reconciliation, financing constraints and DCF domain restrictions. Tests include
an independent constant-cash-flow answer, the preserved Lab 05/06 training-case
answer, valuation monotonicity, the equity bridge, invalid inputs, corrupted
cash, exhausted financing and driver-panel recovery. Inline-XBRL extraction
checks seven opening/denominator inputs against the saved filing. These tests
do not validate all source data or the economic plausibility of the forecasts.

## Remaining decision limitations

- Later quarterly information and the interim valuation-date timing have not
  been incorporated. Full-year forecasts cannot silently be treated as a
  valuation-date stub forecast.
- A filing-to-normalized-base income/cash-flow bridge and treatment of one-time
  items are still needed; opening balance equality is not that reconciliation.
- Source-backed EV peer multiples, updated share dilution and other claims,
  terminal reinvestment, and the final assumption/peer challenge record remain
  incomplete. The existing Lab 08 P/E comparison alone is not the full requirement.
- The case range is conditional model output, not a confidence interval. The
  narrower operating-driver ranges and the broader WACC range in the interface
  are exploration choices requiring final evidence support.
- An initiation decision requires an evidence-supported downside relative to
  a date-consistent market price and a chosen holding horizon. No threshold or
  expected return has been fabricated to convert this draft into a trade call.
